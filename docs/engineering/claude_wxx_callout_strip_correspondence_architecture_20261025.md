# Plan callout to physical glazing strip correspondence: architecture report (shadow only)

Status: default-off, shadow-only source receipt. Nothing here grants a physical opening, host, opening kind, frame, scale, metric, count, quantity or customer output. Written under `AGENTS.md` ("first deliverable on a new change") and the 2026-10-24 owner policy; claim on the coordination hub #3462 (comment 6387728853). The module, tests and census tool in this PR implement the design below after an adversarial review (section 4).

## 1. Observed repository and drawing behavior

**The binder.** `ScheduleOpeningInstanceBindingProducer.publish_scope` asks `physical_opening_authority().prove_existence(selector)` for an already-proven physical opening, derives its aperture with `_opening_aperture_for_physical_opening`, and binds a plan tag only when `_aperture_contains_bbox(aperture, tag_bbox)` holds (the tag bbox *centre* inside the jamb-bounded aperture). A tag outside every aperture gets `schedule_opening_instance_binding_no_contained_tag`.

**Original Maryborough, SHA-256 `b1be5353…e007`, plan p7 (A110, /Rotate 90, native coordinates unrotated) and elevation p29 (A610):**
- The window objects on p7 are *glazing strips*: chains of panels (innermost thin pane box inside an outer frame cell, closed across its full depth at both panel ends) separated by posts, with end posts. G17 sees the post gaps (2.6 to 2.9 pt) and 330 generic jamb-bounded interruptions, not the strips.
- Each window callout is a closed convex six-edge 0.48 pt hexagon with the native `Wxx` word inside and no leader. The door callouts `D01`..`D28` have no hexagon (they are not six-edge rings).
- All strip strokes are black, one exact width (0.48 pt). Page coordinates lie on a 0.24 pt lattice.
- p29 carries the type frames. The existing `OpeningElevationFrameAreaProducer` returns a record for W01, W02 and W05 only (W03 and W04 give none; W06 gives one whose figured width disagrees with its drawn frame, and its strip is single-panel). The record labels axes by *native* x/y and has no rotation field; on a /Rotate 90 page the display-horizontal width is the native-y one.
- On `main`, text trust is `abstained` for every p7/p29 word (`text_glyph_mapping_unverified`, `text_clip_state_unresolved`), so the default producer yields no callout word at all. The opt-in of #3859 (`source_native_finish_text_shadow`) is the prerequisite. The producer's visible-segment universe also drops every segment shorter than 0.5 pt, which hides the 0.48 pt glass pair of the single-panel W06.

## 2. Inference (labelled)

1. A callout-to-strip association cannot be proven from the hexagon alone (there is no leader). It can be *corroborated* by facts the drafter did not draw for that purpose: the strip's measured panel count, and an overall length that agrees with the tagged type's printed width under one plan/elevation ratio shared by several windows.
2. Panel count is a gate, not a discriminator (three 3-panel strips on the page). Length is the discriminator, and the only honest tolerance is the one the drawing's own quanta define.
3. Location (the callout beside the strip) is a *necessary* condition. It can only remove a candidate. It never selects one and never breaks a tie.

## 3. Proposed change (as implemented)

New files only: `pb_source_plan_glazing_strip_callout_shadow.py`, `tools/diag_plan_callout_strip_census.py`, tests, this report. No existing module is edited.

**Inputs are producer-owned.** `audit_plan_callout_to_glazing_strip_from_source(source, revision_id, ...)` takes a `SourceVisibilityProducer` and a published revision: it checks the current revision, that both pages are decoded, and that the producer's own revision bytes match the published SHA-256; it reads trusted words through the producer's text-integrity authority (CORROBORATED + `TRUSTED_PDF_TEXT` only), visible ink through `resolve_visible` (stroke width/colour joined from the producer's own primitive of the same id, geometry compared exactly), elevation type frames through the sealed `OpeningElevationFrameAreaRecord`, and plan viewports through `segment_page_viewports` (RESOLVED only). A page with no usable segmentation is admitted whole for diagnostics only: the strip inventory and positions are reported, but that fallback is *not* a resolved viewport, so every callout ABSTAINS `plan_viewport_not_resolved` and the page carries `plan_viewport_whole_page_unsegmented`. Every record carries document, revision, snapshot, source SHA-256, page and viewport ownership and every evidence id is a producer observation id. A pure core (`audit_plan_callout_to_glazing_strip_shadow`) works on already-extracted records so synthetic tests need no PDF.

**Strip inventory (page wide, independent of every callout).** Strokes are grouped by *exact* width (no page median). Coordinates are snapped by single-linkage clustering at a quarter of the page's coordinate quantum (a scope parameter, 0.24 pt here, measured from the source). A panel is an innermost closed pane box inside an enclosing frame cell where the glass pair is narrower than each rebate, is centred, and the cell is closed across its full depth at both ends; collinear pieces are unioned first, so splitting and double ink do not matter. Panels chain only on identical frame offsets with a gap no wider than the frame depth. A strip's extent runs jamb to jamb and is *incomplete* (excluded from length tests) when an end post is missing. Axis-aligned native H/V ink only.

**Callout ring.** One closed convex six-edge cycle of black ink around the word centre (containment decided on the centre alone, endpoints snapped at the same quarter-quantum); none, or two, abstains / conflicts. Vertices with fewer than two neighbours are pruned first and the search has a deterministic path-expansion budget (past it the word abstains with `callout_ring_search_budget_exceeded`; an adversarial 22-vertex complete graph, 231 edges, would otherwise enumerate about 8.9 million length-six paths). The white mask is not required: hidden duplicate ink is already removed by producer visibility.

**Conditions (tri-state; CANDIDATE needs every one `passed`, enforced at construction):** tag unique on the plan page (counted over all trusted native words by type key, whatever the report pattern: `W01` and `W1` are one key); ring unique; callout in exactly one resolved viewport; exactly one elevation type record; its width resolved (display-horizontal, page-rotation aware, axis label verified against the frame box, ambiguity abstains); panel count measurable (at least 2: single-panel fingerprints abstain); a count-matching strip exists; exactly one *scale-consistent* strip; all chosen pairs form one common ratio group; fingerprint exclusive across tags; callout beside that strip; no other callout ring claims it; strip and callout in the same viewport.

**Scale-consistent.** A (type, strip) pair is supported when a pair of another type on another strip has the same ratio of printed width to drawn extent within the sum of the two pairs' own tolerances, each `2 x coordinate quantum / extent + dimension print step / width`. The ratios are computed internally for this consistency test only; none is exported, published or granted, no title-block scale text is read, no second scale resolver exists, and fewer than two eligible types abstain. Pairwise support alone is not a shared ratio, so the chosen pair of every type must also fall in exactly one mutually consistent group: two internally consistent but incompatible groups, a non-clique group, or a pair with no partner cannot claim one shared ratio (`competing_ratio_groups` CONFLICT, `ratio_group_unshared` ABSTAINED).

**Exclusivity runs over every ring around any native word that could label an opening** (the repository's own tag syntax, plus untrusted words), not over the tags or pattern a caller reports on; the pattern only selects what is reported. A ring that cannot be decided (two rings, search budget, unbounded ink) stays an exclusion-only uncertain claimant by its word's position, whether the word is trusted or not (`strip_has_unresolved_ring_claimant`). Any second claimant, in either direction, makes everything CONFLICT with all rivals retained (`count_matching`, `scale_consistent`, `position` strips and `rival_callout_ids`). **Untrusted native words are handled fail-closed:** the adapter passes their boxes (never their text) and a word that sits inside a ring beside a candidate strip, or whose ring search is undecidable and whose centre lies beside the strip, is an *unverified-label claimant*; the candidate then ABSTAINS with `strip_has_claimant_with_untrusted_label` because its exclusivity cannot be proven. A trusted second claimant still gives CONFLICT. Vocabulary is `EvidenceResolutionStatus` plus reason codes; ids use `stable_contract_id`; all twelve grant flags are False and enforced in `__post_init__`; `text_trust_verified_by_this_module` is False.

**Not claimed:** that the drafter intended the strip, that it is a physical opening or host or a window kind, that an elevation frame equals the plan opening, a scale, any instance count (a type mark labels a type), and anything for single-panel strips. Consumption (a binder variant, a strip-level physical-opening authority) is out of scope and needs its own authority review.

## 4. Review outcome (five independent critics; the verification phase did not run)

Five critic lenses reported (authority/contract, fingerprint/scale, "nearest in disguise", geometry robustness, rivals/completeness), all *sound with changes*. The verification pass was cut off by a session limit, so none of the findings was independently adjudicated; each was weighed by me against the repository and the drawing. Adopted: producer-owned inputs and ownership fields; no fitted or published scale; tri-state conditions and no CANDIDATE with an unevaluated length; location demoted to a filter; exclusivity over all rings; single-panel abstention; lattice-derived tolerance (no page median, no 0.011 constant); exact-width classes; collinear union; closure/ordinal/centred panel gates; one eq for rings and strips; centre-only word containment; display-width via page rotation with ambiguity abstention; grants enforced at construction; import-boundary and constant-hygiene tests. Not adopted, with reason: per-panel sequence comparison (the producer record has no per-panel lengths); a pen class tied to the ring's strokes (would couple the census to the callouts); an "elevation-internal printed vs drawn width" check (W06 only, and W06 abstains on panel count anyway); re-deriving the lattice per page (kept as a recorded scope parameter the census tool documents); non-glazing neighbours and G17 overlaps as passive ids (not built); a requirement that the hexagon lie inside one panel's along-range (6/6 development windows satisfy it, so it would be a fitted condition). Known limits kept visible: claimants are hexagon rings that enclose a native word (a ring whose tag is not a native word is not inventoried; the page carries `callout_rings_without_native_words_not_inventoried`), the D callouts are bubbles and are not modelled (checked on the original: no door-tag word lies within 500 pt of any candidate strip), the inventory covers this anatomy only (many single-panel frames on the page are not inventoried), and elevation marks printed outside the type frame are carried as a caveat, not a block. Three rounds of exact-head review of #3869 produced nine defects, all fixed on the same PR: a whole-page fallback counted as a resolved viewport; the report pattern hiding a duplicate type key; unbounded ring search; exclusivity passing despite untrusted rival labels; trusted undecidable rings vanishing from exclusivity; the target pattern narrowing the claimant universe; a receipt that accepted an empty or partial condition set; pairwise support standing in for one shared ratio; and documentation saying no ratio is computed (it is, internally, and never exported).

## 5. Tests

98 tests (94 run anywhere, 4 original-source skipped without the private drawing), all synthetic and generated with PyMuPDF; none reads a project file. Positives, tri-state/grant/ownership invariants, rotation of the elevation width (0/90/180/270, swapped label, square frame), 1.4 percent twin strips (the quantum tolerance separates them; a post-width-sized bound conflicts, checked), two tags on one strip, twin strips for one tag, lone window, single panel, missing end posts, callout beyond/straddling/over the band, missing and doubled ring, single vs double ink, duplicate tag, door-callout claimant on either side, viewport absent/other/expanded/overlapping, parallel-line / door-leaf / doubled wall / mixed-weight / dimension-tick look-alikes, translation (lattice and sub-lattice shifts), axis swap, uniform scale, 800 thin strokes of unrelated ink, collinear splitting, input order, stroke budget, untrusted-text caveat, the producer adapter through the real producers on a synthetic two-page PDF (plan /Rotate 0 and 90), an import-boundary test, a source-scan test (no project/benchmark/SHA/tag constants, no `page.rect`), and an original-source regression that is skipped ("NOT VERIFIED") unless a private drawing is supplied.

## 6. Original-source result (not a score)

Run through the census tool with the #3859 opt-in, plan p7, elevation p29, pattern `[WD]\d{1,3}`, panel token `FG`, quanta 0.24 pt / 5 mm: 34 callout words, 6 strips (three 3-panel at 204.00 pt H, 206.88 pt V, 232.32 pt V; one 7-panel at 430.08 pt V; two single-panel), **3 CANDIDATE** and 31 ABSTAINED (22 untrusted p7 words, none claims a candidate strip). The candidates are the 204.00 pt, 232.32 pt and 430.08 pt strips; each is supported by the other two with a dimensionless ratio disagreement of 0.06 to 0.07 percent. W03 and W04 abstain (no elevation record); W06 abstains (single panel); the D callouts abstain (no ring). Without the opt-in (today's `main`) there are no trusted callout words and the result is empty: this work is blocked on #3859 and claims nothing before it. These are consistency results under stated hypotheses, not authority, and they change no quantity.
