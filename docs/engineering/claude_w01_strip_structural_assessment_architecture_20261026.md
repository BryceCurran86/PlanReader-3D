## 1. Observed repository behavior (read-only trace, file:line from the five readers)

**`PhysicalOpeningAuthority.prove_existence` takes one selector and nothing else.** It is `prove_existence(selector: ObservationSelector)` (`pb_physical_opening_authority.py:4161`) with `ObservationSelector` = document, revision, SHA-256, snapshot, observation id (`pb_source_observation_authority.py:250-258`). There is deliberately no input channel for candidates: the constructor accepts only the concrete producer-owned readers (`:1230-1250`, `:1410-1422`), candidate discovery is private and memoised per page (`:3349-3365`), and the producer docstring says callers cannot inject observations or candidate sets (`pb_source_visibility_authority.py:1613-1620`). Per selector it re-resolves the current revision, re-hashes the source bytes, checks the snapshot, rebuilds the visible id from its native parent, and batch-authenticates the whole page (`pb_source_observation_authority.py:2068-2163`, `pb_source_visibility_authority.py:3834-4023`, `:3594-3832`).

**What makes `PHYSICAL_OPENING_EXISTS` (VISIBLE mode).** Nine ordered steps (`:4226-4403`): visible resolve CORROBORATED; page records authenticate; candidates are the strong 6-segment pattern (two same-interval breaks on distinct parallel axes plus two jambs that are single visible segments whose endpoints coincide with the break endpoints within 1e-6, six distinct observations each with one distinct parent, no collinear stub strictly inside either gap) union the generic 3/4-observation window/door patterns; viewport scope gate; a single containing candidate; for the strong pattern only, opposition by native dimension caps or white/pale unstroked fills; geometry identity resolved; then CORROBORATED. RAW mode can never exceed CANDIDATE. The record id is geometry-only: `stable_contract_id('physical_opening_existence', {document, revision, sha, page, 'opening', source_geometry})` where `source_geometry` is the sorted six canonical lines rounded to 6 decimals (`:557-612`, `:4366-4394`).

**Terminals are pure centreline topology.** No width, colour, fill, layer, dash, return or flank-ink test exists on the vector path; `SourceObservationRecord` carries geometry, kind and origin only, no stroke width (`pb_source_observation_authority.py:226-247`). A missing jamb gives `visible_wall_continuation_required` with no terminal-specific reason; a jamb split in two or 0.01 pt off breaks the strong candidate while per-post candidates survive; a rectangle pier abutting the faces yields nested strong candidates and total CONFLICT; cropped geometry is dropped by the clip rule and cannot be told from never drawn; hidden geometry is only handled by clip (no optional-content or z-order).

**A multi-panel strip is not representable.** One candidate is one gap between two collinear face breaks; there is no panel, mullion, post or pier concept in the file. Interior perpendicular lines are ignored, so a strip whose outer terminals match exactly is seen as ONE void and its interior mullion abstains; but glazing pieces lying on the face axes inside the gap veto the spanning break (`:2729-2760`) and instead yield per-bay candidates that share members, which resolve to CONFLICT. The only mullion concept in the repo is the unconsumed U2 `regular_parallel_mullion_array` (KIND_GLAZING, OPPOSING). No code relates a small candidate to an enclosing one; post-gap and whole-strip supports are disjoint and would coexist. Today's CORROBORATED post-gap openings already feed W4 SAME_PHYSICAL_WALL overrides when six primitives map to W4 candidates (`pb_physical_wall_candidate_authority.py:2408-2569`).

**Quarantine precedents.** `exact_g17_nomination_shadow` (a strict-bool default-off `SourceVisibilityProducer` argument that changes only opt-in identity versions, with `type(flag) is bool` enforcement and tests rejecting None/1/'true'/[]/{}) and the raster source-policy flags (captured at creation and re-checked per call, `pb_physical_opening_authority.py:1251-1259`, `:4162-4167`). Accessors that take caller-named ids and re-authenticate them already exist (`native_unstroked_fill_evidence`, `native_dimension_annotation_evidence`, `visible_candidate_structures`) and fail closed to `()` capped at CANDIDATE `EvidenceAtom`s. The existing G17 terminal-flank, face-end-role, flank-W4-end-reach and W4-equivalence-competitor shadows are raster-only and unusable on native ids.

**Consumers cannot be reached by an assessment object.** Every consumer takes selectors and re-calls `prove_existence`: the schedule binder needs CORROBORATED + `PHYSICAL_OPENING_EXISTS` + a record and an aperture from exactly six re-resolved visible lines (or raster `aperture_bbox_pt`); the host binder needs equal `record_id`, `PHYSICAL_OPENING_IDENTITY_RESOLVED` and a unique host band; `_opening_quantity` needs an exact `LiveCanonicalOpeningObject`; the count producer needs the source-authenticated semantic seal that the native adapter does not grant; W10 duck-types on `host_candidate_id`/`candidate_wall_ids_considered`. There is no repo-wide gate for "new module imported by production"; each shadow ships its own AST import-boundary test, and no existing test feeds a foreign record type to these consumers (existing foreign-object tests assert acceptance of duck-typed objects).

**Existing negative vocabulary.** Ten kinds, three polarities, free-string methods, status CANDIDATE only from U2. Negatives reach `prove_existence` only for the strong pattern with a producer and only BLOCK (ABSTAINED, candidate attached, atoms in `opposing_evidence_atoms`); they never flip the proposition negative or erase the candidate. `EvidenceAtom` enforces only non-empty document and page ids; revision, snapshot, SHA and source observation ids are metadata by convention (the strongest convention is the native opposition producers' `revision_id`, `source_sha256`, `snapshot_id`, `source_observation_ids`, `polarity`, explicit False grant flags). Atom ids are snapshot-bound while `PhysicalOpeningExistenceRecord.record_id` is geometry-bound and snapshot-free.

**Real-page regime (W01/W02/W05, original SHA `b1be5353…e007`, p7).** Measured read-only through the real producer. CORRECTION (2026-10-26): an earlier version of this paragraph called the already-corroborated fragments "overlapping two-panel spans running past the strip". That measured the extent of each candidate's member lines. The opening of a candidate is the gap between its two jambs, and the corroborated openings inside W01, W02 and W05 are the 2.6 to 2.9 pt POST gaps between panels (plus junction gaps at the strip ends). ## 2. Inference (labelled)

1. A glazing strip is evidence of a drawn filling, not yet a whole structural opening: a mullion, end post, wall pier, return and neighbouring door are different roles. The existing stack cannot say which role a stroke has, because it never sees width, colour or role.
2. Reusing the elevation-width fingerprint both to choose the strip (the nominator) and to establish its physical existence would be circular. Existence is page-coordinate evidence and needs none of the elevation facts.
3. The only safe entry is a nomination by immutable observation ids that the stack re-derives itself; a caller-supplied object (including the #3869 dataclass, which is unsealed) cannot certify provenance.
4. The measured regime (section 6) is the per-bay one for W01, W02 and W05: the existing strong path proves the interior post gaps, not a void bounded at the strip's own terminals. One strip on the page behaves differently (the neighbouring 3-panel strip of 206.88 pt, whose wall faces break once and whose jambs sit at its lo terminal and 0.72 pt beyond its hi terminal), so the assessment must report each opening's signed offset from the terminals as data and decide nothing by tolerance. The already-CORROBORATED fragments are carried as members and rivals, never trusted as the strip.

## 3. Proposed change (shadow only; nothing below is built)

**Composition, not modification.** A new module `pb_strip_structural_assessment_shadow.py` holding a new frozen result type and a read-only class that COMPOSES an existing `PhysicalOpeningAuthority` (obtained only from a `SourceVisibilityProducer`) and the producer's visibility authority. It is not a subclass, adds no method to `PhysicalOpeningAuthority`, and changes none of `prove_existence`, `classify_disposition`, `assess_*_candidate_closure`, `capabilities()` or the existence record. If review decides a method on `PhysicalOpeningAuthority` is needed after all (the smallest existing seam, modelled on `native_unstroked_fill_evidence`), that is a separate, explicitly reviewed production edit.

**Gate.** A strict-bool constructor flag, default False, `type(flag) is bool` else raise; off returns ABSTAINED with a typed reason and touches nothing. No flag is added to `SourceVisibilityProducer`.

**Input.** A selector (document, revision, SHA, snapshot, page) and a sequence of VISIBLE observation ids. Nothing else is accepted and caller strings (`input_provenance`, `plan_viewport_basis`, widths, strip ids) are ignored. Each id is re-resolved through the visibility authority; one page, one snapshot, no empty, duplicate or foreign ids. Page context (collinear-stub vetoes, rival candidates, viewport scope) comes from the authority's own records, not the nominee.

**Stroke role evidence.** Width is read by a producer-side source-role read keyed by `segment:<id>` with exact geometry parity and a `PRODUCER_INTEGRITY_FAILURE` on mismatch (sibling of `native_unstroked_fill_evidence`: `page.get_drawings()` + `extract_native_page`), never from the nominee's `PlanInkSegment.width`. All stroke widths and colours at a terminal are inspected; the assessment states what is drawn (width class, how it joins the band edges, whether it continues past the band) and applies no distance or thickness threshold derived from these windows.

**Terminal and aperture logic (questions, not yet rules).** Establish the whole outer aperture at both ends from independent structural faces, returns and jambs through the wall-depth context; prove a panel post is not a structural boundary; reconcile every G17 post fragment with the whole assembly (retain them as members/rivals, never as the strip); missing, cropped, hidden or contradictory terminals abstain with no inferred ink and no generic minimum-area rule. Duplicate policy: map each nominated id to its geometry class and retain the whole class (do not keep "the last"), so a non-representative duplicate cannot silently fall out. Require a positive authenticated FLOOR_PLAN viewport (do not inherit the authority's pass-through when a page has no authenticated viewport).

**Output.** A new frozen `StripStructuralAssessment` carrying `EvidenceAtom`s with status in {CANDIDATE, ABSTAINED, CONFLICT} only, ids from `stable_contract_id` over the full ownership root (document, revision, SHA, snapshot, page, viewport) plus sorted source observation ids and the nominating strip id; metadata keys `revision_id`, `source_sha256`, `snapshot_id`, `source_observation_ids`, `polarity`, explicit False grant flags (copying #3869's constructor enforcement). Never a `PhysicalOpeningExistenceRecord`, never written to `_visible_existence_cache`, `_visible_candidate_cache`, `_visible_candidate_membership_cache` or `_wall_source_opening_page_proof_cache`, never read by closure or disposition, never memoised by selector alone (a stale-revision CORROBORATED was reproduced from the existing selector memo).

What this establishes (source-only): for W01, W02 and W05 the existing authority proves the interior posts as physical openings and never the strip, and no candidate has its jambs at the strip's terminals; the host face line running continuously through the glazing (GPT1, hub 6398246162) is a consistent explanation but is not tested here. What it does NOT establish: that any post opening is wrong as a wall interruption, how any opening is bounded by wall ink, or what a downstream consumer does with posts or with the one W03 opening. Two consequences for the design: a nomination must carry posts and junction gaps as members and rivals, and the W03 case shows the assessment must report offsets from the terminals as data. The question of which downstream reader (the binder's `_aperture_contains_bbox`, the W4 SAME_PHYSICAL_WALL override, the count seal) sees a post opening today belongs to their owners; I have not traced it past the accessor.

Terminals.
