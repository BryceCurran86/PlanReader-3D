The ledger rejects duplicate physical IDs, frame IDs without hosts,
summary/identity mismatches, extra/missing raw candidate pages,
contradictory or duplicated unresolved raw candidates, out-of-scope records,
and the wrong 20,000 primitive safety bound. It reports per-identity first
blocked host, equivalence or source-frame stage; fully framed identities
are explicitly marked downstream witness/count still UNVERIFIED. It does not
touch G17, W2/W4, W10, count or quantity authority, sealed output, expected
takeoffs, physical scale, source tolerance, frozen V2 manifests, evaluators,
or benchmark scoring.
