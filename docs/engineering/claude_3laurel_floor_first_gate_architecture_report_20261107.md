## 1. Observed repository behavior (SHA-original, Windows, counts only)

1. **The set is a layered CAD export.** Page 3 carries 11,030 native drawings over 19 named optional-content layers (plus 110 unlayered). The existing W2 rules (`_HATCH_`, `_DIMENSION_`, `_TEXT_FRAME_` keywords, marker+section, shell+roof) exclude only the annotation and section-marker layers. The layers they do not touch hold 3,529 joinery, 764 plumbing-fixture, 625 floor-finish, 373 ceiling/bulkhead and about 1,300 service drawings, beside the 2,419 wall-layer drawings (`tools/diag_layered_floor_plan_first_gate.py`).
2. **The existing handoff yields nothing for floors.** `--family-group floor_area --source-plan-page-index 2`: exit 0, about 16 min locally, `status=no_sealable_quantities`; 1,555 walls, 292 room faces, 0 metric-complete; `same_view_room_area` first failures 282 `prerequisites_unavailable`, 7 `label_duplicate`, 2 `dimension_witness_intersection_unavailable`, 1 `trusted_label_ambiguous`; with the repeated-label opt-in 4 `repeated_label_source_observation_ambiguous`; every room `physical_scale_bar_unavailable`.
3. **The faces are not the rooms.** Overlaying the 292 faces on a render shows tile and plank hatch, fixtures and shower symbols fragmenting the wet rooms into slivers and diagonal-edged polygons. The one closed, labelled wet-room face follows fixture and niche linework, and its area is about 27% below the area of its own bounding box. The main-house bedrooms have no face at all.
4. **Excluding the polluting layers does not create rooms (experiment, monkeypatch only, nothing committed).** Adding `interior`, `finish` and `service` to the W2 non-wall layer token sets: 1,555 -> 604 walls, 292 -> 37 faces, all tiny; still no bedroom or wet-room face. With the pollution gone, a render of the wall layers alone shows each room I rendered (two bedrooms, one granny-flat wet room) with its ring **open at its doorway**: the door leaf and swing are drawn on the wall layer and no wall edge closes the gap.
5. **Printed figured dimensions and wall faces are the same geometry.** Of 90 authenticated native figured dimensions on the page, 72 have **both** extents exactly (<= 0.05 pt) on a face of a filled wall-layer polygon; in a ray-cast prototype (not committed) the horizontal clear span between the wall-layer faces of two bedrooms agrees with the printed figure to within 1 mm; rays that meet door, sill or window linework do not, and are not counted as corroboration. This is corroboration of finished-face placement, not a measurement.
6. **The dedicated wet-area detail sheets do not help today.** Pages 11, 12, 13 and 15 (1:50 plans plus elevations, no frames) segment into **zero** viewports; each is one page scope with 663 to 1,967 wall candidates; `compose_live_canonical_rooms` abstains with `source_room_face_duplicate_edge_ownership` (three sheets) or `source_room_face_tiny_or_degenerate` (one): the room-face pollution guard (`_derive_scope_outcome`, withheld faces >= half of all faces) rejects the whole scope because the elevations share it.
7. **There is no finish schedule in this set.** Finishes are plan annotations under each room name. `CrossViewFloorFinishProducer` needs an authenticated schedule definition plus an occurrence, so it cannot issue a finish row here; the honest first row is `floor_area`, and a finish row needs a new, separately reviewed evidence class.
8. **Internal openings.** A numeric word census over all 18 pages finds no printed door height. The 18 internal opening items cannot reach FIRM without target-owned height: ABSTAIN, as the handover requires.

## 2. Inference

- The first gate for every 3Laurel floor, tile and bedroom case is a **closed, finished-face room ring**, not scale, labels, dimensions or finish. Three independent causes: fixture/hatch layers admitted as wall linework (1,3), open rings at doorways (4), and unframed multi-view sheets sharing one room-face scope (6).
- Layer exclusion alone (P1) is necessary but not sufficient (4).
- The same doorway-closure input is what Maryborough OFFICE D15 needs; a general seam serves both and should be built once.

## 7. Addendum: the doorway-closed ring shadow (built and measured, same day)

Claim: [#4135 comment 6700881682](https://github.com/BryceCurran86/PlanReader-3D/issues/4135#issuecomment-6700881682). New files only: `pb_layered_wall_doorway_room_ring_shadow.py` and `tests/test_layered_wall_doorway_room_ring_shadow_v1.py`; the census tool gained `--rings`. Imported by no live path; status CANDIDATE, `takeoff_eligible=False`, `commercial_projection_allowed=False`; no room identity, scale, area, finish or quantity is issued.

**What it does.** From one SHA-pinned page's native drawings it takes filled black strips on an explicit wall layer (reusing `pb_wall_layer_evidence_shadow.is_explicit_wall_layer_name`, so a wall FINISH layer is not a wall), finds every bounded interruption of a wall band, and closes only those with source evidence: a swing doorway (one circular quarter-arc plus its leaf stroke, whose closed-door segment lies inside the interruption and on the wall line within one wall thickness) or a source stroke that spans the interruption end to end. The doorway is the maximal interruption containing the closed-door segment, so it is unique by construction (two swings in one gap, or one swing matching two gaps, close nothing). Rings are the bounded free-space regions left after the closures are added as barriers; a closure is a planarization barrier only, never a wall. Edges are split at closure boundaries so each ring edge is wholly wall or wholly doorway chord, which is the shape the existing verifier `evaluate_source_room_boundary_doorway_shadow` (#3426) needs; the tests run that verifier over a produced ring.

**What it deliberately does not do.** A wall band interval that ends at a perpendicular wall without a collinear wall on its far side is indistinguishable from the open side of a free-standing return, so it is neither closed nor asserted open; whether a region is one room is decided downstream by label, figured-box and doorway evidence. Cavity sliders, vertical sliders and open passages have no closure evidence here and stay open. Non-rectangular wall fills are kept as barriers and counted.

**Original, page 3 (counts only).** 203 wall strips; 28 closures (6 swing, 22 source stroke); 2 swing symbols unplaced and 2 ambiguous (kept open); 12 collinear unresolved gaps; 3 sliver regions discarded (narrower than the page's median wall thickness); 4 enclosed regions: 3 CANDIDATE and 1 ABSTAINED (`open_wall_gap_unresolved`). Of the 3 candidates, **one bedroom ring has a bounding box equal (<= 0.5 pt) to exactly one authenticated figured (horizontal, vertical) pair**: a physical finished-face ring, closed by a source swing doorway, corroborated by printed figures. A twin bedroom has the same ring but its horizontal figure is not among the authenticated dimensions, so no tie; the third candidate is a merged multi-room region (it leaks through openings with no closure evidence) with no figured box. A second, shorter figured pair exists whose box is smaller than the bedroom ring (it stops at the wardrobe line); the ring selects the larger pair, and which extent a finish covers is a finish-zone question this shadow does not decide.

**Tests (26 + 9).** Positive ring with swing and stroke closures; independent verifier accepts every edge, and rejects the same polygon without the chord; no evidence, leafless or non-circular arc, floating swing, two swings in one gap, swing wider than the gap or off the wall line (vertical and horizontal), short or half stroke: all stay open; non-wall and finish layers claim nothing; hairline sliver; jamb caps (single and chained) and thin lining pieces do not fake an interruption; free-standing return stays a candidate with the return as a notch; translation, 90-degree rotation, uniform scale, wall-strip splitting, input order, far unrelated content, two rooms sharing a door; deterministic replay and stable ids; no mutation; producer-owned records and input validation; digest mismatch refusal. A 15-mutant harness (exact-anchor replace and restore) leaves no survivor.

**Still not done (the wiring).** The existing chain consumes rings only through `SourceRoomFaceAuthority`. Feeding this record there needs (1) a closure owner kind in `pb_source_room_face_authority._derive_scope_outcome` (an edge with no wall owner is today a whole-scope block), (2) the W2/W4 wall-candidate input for that scope restricted by layer (default-off, protected replay of Lot16 eight and Maryborough three first), and (3) the label binding for the ring. All three are shared files; each needs its own exact claim and owner.
