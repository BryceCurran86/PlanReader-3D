# 3Laurel: physical cavity-slider pocket wall vs. sliding leaf

**Source interpretation confirmed by Bryce on the marked plan (16 Nov 2026):**
yellow markup denotes the **physical wall**, pink markup denotes the
**cavity sliding door**. These are separate roles, even when the slider
pocket fits *inside* the wall framing. Do not substitute the sliding
leaf/track for a solid wall, and do not erase wall construction around
the pocket. The actual clear passage remains a **doorway threshold**;
floor finishes stop at the relevant marked threshold and wall-area
quantities exclude the opening while retaining genuine wall portions
above/around the frame when independently height/schedule authenticated.

`pb_three_laurel_native_slider_wall_end_correspondence_shadow.py`
does not interpret the user's screenshot pixels or create a new door
detector. It correlates **already detected source-native** candidate
`CS` / paired sliding-frame openings with **two separate original black
fill wall-end path IDs**. The two physical wall owners must each be on
a named original `Enclosure_WallInternal` or `Enclosure_WallExternal`
layer, aligned on the same wall band, meet opposite gap endpoints,
be uniquely identified, share compatible native viewport and never
be a solid black fill across the clear opening. An annotation, colored
user markup or an isolated pink shape cannot supply missing native wall
geometry. Contradictions abstain.

A separate read-only adapter in
`pb_three_laurel_floor_layered_closure_shadow.py` exposes
`independently_painted_wall_end_path_ids` alongside the existing
`evidence_completeness` for the ORIGINAL closure producer. This
intentionally does **not** upgrade `word_only_unhosted` or
`native_leaf_without_host_wall_ids` to producer-level authentication:
the independently found wall ends do not prove all original leaf, cap,
frame, partition return, finish, scale and commercial responsibilities.
