# G17 / ordinary W4 cross-source equivalence: fail-closed original competitor census

## Current first authority failure

Original G17 `raster_opening_primitive:` face/end observations and the ordinary page W4 `raster_segment:` detector derive from a raster source but are **different provenance namespaces**. The existing source-only shadow authenticates original image ink and isolated W2/W3/W4 candidates; it cannot prove that a wall produced from one namespace is the same physical structural wall as one in the other. Identical or overlapping pixel geometry is not source ancestry, structural role or unique physical identity.

## Implemented scope

`pb_g17_w4_equivalence_competitor_shadow.py` enumerates every **currently produced** ordinary W4 source-edge record alongside each independently authenticated source-solid G17 opening-local flank. It retains every *exact-collinear positive-overlap* candidate and its source primitive IDs, including ambiguous or duplicated W4 identities; reports all unmatched G17 source edges, direct provenance matches if any, and graph-address collisions. It never uses first, nearest, numeric opening width, toleranced wall-body proximity, inferred raster scale or benchmark truth.

The original-source diagnostic obtains both sides from the same real `SourceVisibilityProducer` and `compose_live_wall_opening_authority`, compares only the existing producer-owned W4 records, then **recomposes** and checks exact wall/host/frame/canonical-area receipt identity. This is not a producer authority entrypoint; the pure overlap helper cannot grant a wall.

## Mandatory abstentions and next production change

Even a unique exact geometric overlap or shared string source address remains **non-authoritative** without independent actual W3 structural-role and wall-equivalence proof from the complete competing source-owned wall universe. Outputs explicitly preserve `physical_wall_role_granted=false`, `physical_wall_equivalence_granted=false`, `host_binding_granted=false`, `metric_quantity_granted=false`, and `ordinary_w4_universe_complete_proven=false`. External source bodies cannot enter the diagnostic’s source construction.

The original Lot16 page-3 CI requires exact source SHA, 21 existence claims, 15 authenticated hosts, nine frames, 2,094 ordinary W4, eight original areas, all 12 G17 flank comparisons, and exact before/after receipt parity. These figures are CI retention controls only, never algorithm parameters. The frozen V2 evaluator, truth, denominators, customer publisher, source snapshot, W2 global 2.5pt snap and 20,000 safety cap remain unchanged.
