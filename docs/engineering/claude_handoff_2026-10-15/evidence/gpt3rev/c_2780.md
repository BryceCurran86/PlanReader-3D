- Wall time halves (2.0x) on the runner, so the potential is real: `pytest -q tests/` is ~90 % of the main CI job.

The *extra* xdist failures are different on the two Python versions, which is the signature of shared mutable state between test processes:
  - 3.13: `test_autopilot_model_rerun_stability.py` (3 tests) and `test_quick3d_refresh_mass_identity.py` (2) - `sqlite3.IntegrityError: UNIQUE constraint failed: workspaces.id / documents.id`.
