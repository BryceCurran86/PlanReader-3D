**Inference**
- The detector answers 'is there an exact-coordinate U-shaped break between two collinear runs' not 'is there a wall opening'. Abutting wall-piece polygons (seams), pier/box rectangles, joinery squares, floor-finish strips and the graphic scale bar share the exact topology of a window gap, so 139 records for ~41 openings is expected behavior of the rule, not noise.
- Existence is promoted to CORROBORATED (confidence 1.0) at G17 from geometry only. AGENTS.md says detection is not authority; this is the point where it becomes one for openings.
- Identity is the first broken seam, upstream of wall scope (scope_complete=False) and host binding: even with a perfect wall universe only 75 records would be offered, of which 18 are control-matched; 6 real openings that did produce a record are lost purely by the min(id) representative rule, and 8 more never become a record because every support is shared.
- Two independent defects compound: (1) no physical-opening equivalence class (nested face pairs of one opening = several records; ~2.4 records per physical cell on page 3); (2) shared-segment ambiguity is treated as conflict even when the sharers are the same opening, then representative choice by smallest hash id drops the record. This violates the spirit of 'retain all plausible candidates, never resolve ties by first/smallest'.
- The legacy-detector closure (0 of 139 subsumed) means physical_opening_universe_complete is unreachable here regardless of identity fixes; the two detector families are never reconciled. The correct outcome for completeness on this page remains abstain.
- Scale-free geometric signals that can be computed without control input: gap-to-face-separation ratio, face-separation vs a page-derived wall-pair band, preserved layer/fill/width provenance, spanning in-gap infill, and support-sharing/gap-interval equivalence. On this page (control used only to score): ratio >1.5 kept 18/18 matched and 32/33 control-evidenced records while removing 21 of 42 others; the page-derived band [0.4,2.5]x median separation kept 33/33 and removed 16 of 42; both together kept 32/33 and removed 27 of 42, leaving the plausibly real unlabelled doorways. In-gap spanning infill is weak (matched 16/18, seam bucket 7/21) and blind to open doorways. None of these is validated; thresholds here are illustrations measured on one page and must not be tuned to it.
- Layer ids cleanly flag the non-wall look-alikes on this PDF, but names are document-specific; they are admissible only as typed negative evidence atoms on retained candidates (Priority 3), never as a hard filter or a per-project string rule.
- PR #1207 text callouts ('1218 SGW') identify type/size but do not establish physical existence or location; they cannot substitute for fixing G17 identity and should be reconciled against equivalence classes, not individual records.
